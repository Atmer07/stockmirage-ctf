📖 How The Crime Happened — Full Narrative

1 March 2025 — The Hook

Ramesh Kulkarni, a 47-year-old Chartered Accountant from Andheri West, receives a WhatsApp group invite link from an unknown number. The group is named "Mumbai Elite Traders 🚀 | SEBI Tips". The group already has 248 members (all fake/bot accounts). A "stock expert" called Dr. Arvind Kapoor posts daily "free tips" with screenshots of huge gains. Ramesh, curious about stock markets, stays in the group.

🔑 Social Engineering Tactic: The group creates a false sense of legitimacy using SEBI branding, multiple fake member profiles, and testimonials with fabricated profit screenshots.

2 March 2025 — The Romance/Trust Building

A profile named "Priya Sharma" (+91-80762-14398) with an attractive display picture messages Ramesh privately. She says she is a financial analyst at a Mumbai firm, also part of the group. Over 3 days she builds rapport — discussing family, work, markets. She shares she made ₹2.3 lakhs using a "premium trading platform" called TradePro India, which is exclusively invite-only.

🔑 Technique — Pig Butchering: Like fattening a pig before slaughter, scammers invest time building deep trust before asking for money.

5 March 2025 — The Bait (App Download)

Priya sends Ramesh a link to download the TradePro India APK directly (not from Play Store — red flag). She helps him register, and a fake "Relationship Manager" named Vikram Joshi calls him from an Indian VoIP number. Ramesh is onboarded. The app looks professional — it shows live stock prices (scraped from NSE), a portfolio dashboard, and an exciting UI. Priya shows him her own "portfolio" of ₹4.8 lakhs in profit.

8 March 2025 — First Small Win

Ramesh is guided to make his first investment of just ₹5,000 (via UPI). Within 2 days, the app shows a profit of ₹8,400 — a 68% gain. He withdraws successfully (scammers allow first withdrawal to build confidence). Ramesh is hooked.

⚠️ Classic Trick: Scammers always allow the first small withdrawal. This "proof of concept" eliminates all doubt and hooks the victim completely.

10–13 March 2025 — Escalating Investments

Emboldened, Ramesh invests ₹75,000 on March 10. The app shows ₹1,42,000 within 3 days. Priya urges him to invest more before a "special IPO window" closes. On March 13, Ramesh transfers ₹3,00,000 from his savings. The app now shows his portfolio at ₹5,28,000.

15 March 2025 — The Squeeze Begins (Fake Taxes/Fees)

Ramesh tries to withdraw his profits. The app shows an error: "Your account is flagged. You must pay 18% GST on profits before withdrawal as per SEBI mandate." Priya "helps" him — saying it happened to her too and she paid up, then received everything. Ramesh transfers ₹2,00,000 to a "GST collection account" (another mule).

17 March 2025 — RBI Audit Scam

Still unable to withdraw, Ramesh is told his account is under "RBI Audit" due to large transactions. A fake "RBI representative" (scammer) calls from a spoofed number, speaks official Hindi, and says he needs to pay a ₹1,65,000 "audit clearance fee" which will be refunded within 4 hours. Ramesh, desperate to recover his money, pays. Total loss now: ₹8,40,000.

20 March 2025 — Vanishing Act

All WhatsApp contacts go dark. The TradePro India app shows "Server Maintenance." The website goes offline. Ramesh calls his bank — money is gone from all 4 mule accounts, transferred further. He files a complaint at Andheri Cyber Cell on 21 March 2025.

📌 Session Overview

This CTF exercise is designed to follow a 45–60 minute awareness session on Pig Butchering Scams and Investment Fraud. It is best run as a team exercise (teams of 2-3 officers) with 60 minutes to solve all 6 challenges. The exercise reinforces digital forensics skills critical to cybercrime investigation.

🎯 Learning Objectives
  • Understand the anatomy of a Pig Butchering / Investment Fraud operation
  • Learn how to read and interpret WHOIS records for domain-based investigations
  • Practice Base64 decoding as a basic cryptography skill
  • Trace UPI/banking transactions through mule account chains
  • Perform mobile app permission audits for malware identification
  • Extract and interpret image EXIF metadata as forensic evidence
  • Correlate IP geolocation data with international cybercrime networks
⏱ Suggested Session Plan
  • 0:00 – 0:45 — Awareness lecture: How Pig Butchering works, case studies from Cyber Cell Mumbai, legal framework (IPC 420, IT Act 66C/66D, Prevention of Money Laundering Act)
  • 0:45 – 0:50 — Form teams (2-3 officers per team). Distribute this CTF platform.
  • 0:50 – 1:45 — Teams work through challenges. Trainers circulate. Do NOT give direct answers — guide with questions.
  • 1:45 – 2:00 — Debrief. Reveal answers. Discuss investigative steps each flag represents.
  • 2:00 – 2:15 — Q&A: How to actually file MLAT requests, how to work with CERT-In, how to freeze mule accounts within the 1930 golden hour.
🔑 Key Training Points to Emphasize
  • The Golden Hour: Money moved through mule accounts can be frozen if reported to 1930 (National Cybercrime Helpline) within 1 hour. Every minute matters.
  • Never install APKs outside Play Store: Emphasize this to victims during awareness — sideloading is a massive red flag.
  • VoIP Numbers are not traceable via normal channels: Teach officers to use TRAI's VoIP database and how to file a request to the telecom provider.
  • SEBI/RBI never ask for payments: This is a hard rule. Any call claiming to be from a regulator asking for money is a scam.
  • Pig Butchering is a global crime: Most operations run from SEA (Cambodia, Myanmar, Laos). Cases require MLAT (Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty) requests to prosecute operators.
  • Image metadata is evidence: EXIF data from images shared by scammers can reveal device info, location, and timezone. Always extract before evidence is deleted.
📞 Key Resources to Share With Officers
  • 1930 — National Cybercrime Helpline (freeze fraudulent transactions)
  • cybercrime.gov.in — National Cybercrime Reporting Portal
  • CERT-In — cert-in.org.in (for domain/IP investigation support)
  • RBI's UDGAM Portal — Trace unclaimed bank accounts
  • I4C (Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre) — For inter-state and international cases
